Getting there
& staying.

Our ceremony will begin at 2:45 pm and will take place at El Gran Jardin. Please arrive at 2:00pm. After we say I do we’ll celebrate with drinks & canapés at cocktail hour, before moving inside for dinner, speeches, and dancing the night away!

Hotel Piedra Viva is approximately a 1.5-hour drive from Mexico City. For convenience, we recommend accommodation at the hotel. Due to limited capacity, priority will be given to close family members.

Pricing per night is $2,250 MXN for 2 guests (approximately $180 CAD). For double suites, a 3rd and 4th guest are an additional $850 MXN per person. Tepoztlán is also home to many wonderful nearby boutique hotels, and we’ve listed several recommended options below for your convenience.

Airport
Benito Juárez International Airport in Mexico City.

Mexico City to Tepoztlán
Tepoztlán is approximately a 1.5-hour drive from the city, making it easily accessible by private transfer, taxi, Uber or car rental. Shuttle services will be available closer to the date. Please stay tuned.

Tepoztlán to venue
Tepoztlán is a small town, with all accommodation being close to the venue. The best method of transport will be via shuttle, Uber or taxi.

Parking

A week in

We’ve chosen our wedding dates to fall just after Día de los Muertos (November 1st), one of Mexico’s most meaningful and beautiful celebrations. If you are able, we would love for you to celebrate this day with us in Mexico City.

For accommodation, we recommend staying in Roma Norte, Condesa, Reforma, or Centro Histórico, all well-located and easy to explore. Don't hesitate to contact us for more tips.
