Getting there
& staying.

Hotel Piedra Viva
Ocotitlán 4, 62525 Tepoztlán, Morelos, México. Complimentary parking is available.

Fly into Benito Juárez International Airport (MEX). Tepoztlán is approximately 1.5 hours from Mexico City by taxi, Uber or rental car. Buses also run regularly from Taxqueña station.

We recommend Hotel Piedra Viva for convenience. Because rooms are limited, the hotel will prioritize close family. Tepoztlán has many beautiful nearby stays approximately 17 minutes from the venue.

Shuttle information will be shared closer to the date. Uber and taxis are available locally, and all recommended accommodation options are close to the venue.

Expect daytime temperatures around 27°C and evenings near 12°C. Bring a light layer for the cooler mountain air after sunset.
